Critical Threats Solved

  • Kidnapping Attempts Detects sudden movement stops or path deviations in unsafe zones.
  • Physical Attacks Triggers alerts during impact spikes or distress noise thresholds.
  • Accidents & Vehicle Crashes Registers rapid deceleration anomalies and sudden stops.
  • Unresponsive Emergencies Monitors safety states when users are unable to reach their phones.

How NETRA Protects You

  • Situation Analysis AI monitors sensors to differentiate normal movement from sudden distress.
  • Smart Emergency Verification Verifies genuine issues to prevent false alarms for safety responders.
  • Zero-Touch Warning broadcast Automatically shares live location and audio data with trusted contacts.
  • Fast Assistance Routing Sends priority rescue requests to close responders and emergency systems.
